A progress bar for a capture zone. It fills while Terrorists stand in the zone, stops and turns red while both teams are in it, and stays hidden until someone starts capturing.

## How it works

- Four times a second the script lists pawns with <ScriptRef name="IPointScript.FindEntitiesByClass" /> `("player")` and counts the living ones within `RADIUS` units of the marker, per team (2 is Terrorist, 3 is Counter-Terrorist).
- The bar is ten fixed panels, each with an `On` class. An animated `width` would be shorter to write, but community authors report that a width transition restarts whenever a dialog variable in the same subtree changes, and the label here changes while capturing.
- The script remembers what it last sent (segments, state and label) and sends only changes.

## Make it yours

- To use a trigger volume instead of a radius, connect its `OnStartTouch` and `OnEndTouch` outputs with `Instance.ConnectOutput` and keep a set of the pawns inside.
- To end the round on capture, call `GameRules.TerminateRound` when `progress` reaches 1.
